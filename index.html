Our Autonomous Hydrofoil USV

What It Is

A multirole hydrofoil trimaran autonomous surface vessel with T-shaped hydrofoils and hybrid-electric propulsion, designed for persistent maritime operations with minimal crew requirements.

Core Capabilities

Operates continuously with integrated sensor payloads. Transmits real-time data to remote operators for oversight and decision-making while maintaining autonomous capability.

Why It Matters

Hydrofoils are designed to reduce drag, with the goal of extended range and endurance.

Platform Architecture

Multirole hydrofoil trimaran with T-shaped hydrofoils and hybrid-electric propulsion

Planned Capabilities

Designed to enable autonomous navigation, waypoint management, and sensor payload integration

Human Oversight

Designed to maintain remote operator monitoring and intervention capability

Development Focus

Hydrodynamic validation, propulsion integration, and autonomy systems

Our Business Hypothesis

Autonomous platforms can reduce operational costs and personnel risk in maritime environments. Advanced hydrodynamic design minimizes power consumption, extending operational endurance.

Dina Dynamics is developing and validating this platform through prototype iterations.
