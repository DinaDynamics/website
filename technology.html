Technology Overview

Hydrofoil-Based Autonomous Maritime Platform

Autonomous Hydrofoil USV Architecture

Our platform is being designed as a multirole hydrofoil trimaran autonomous surface vessel with T-shaped hydrofoils and hybrid-electric propulsion. The system is designed to integrate advanced hydrodynamics, onboard autonomy, power management, communications, and mission-specific payloads into a cohesive platform.

The architecture prioritizes operational endurance, reliability, autonomous capability, and human-operator integration. Hydrodynamic efficiency is a core design principle, with the goal of minimizing power consumption and extending range.

Autonomous Navigation & Control

The platform is being designed to enable navigation within defined operational areas, waypoint management, and real-time obstacle avoidance. The system is designed to maintain human-operator oversight and intervention capability, with remote monitoring of system status, mission data, and environmental conditions.

This approach is intended to balance autonomous capability with operational safety, human oversight, and regulatory compliance for maritime operations.

Design Philosophy: Practical Hydrodynamics

Dina Dynamics' approach emphasizes hydrodynamic efficiency as a foundation for operational endurance and cost-effectiveness. Our design integrates proven marine engineering principles with modern autonomy, power systems, and control technologies.

The platform is designed for modularity and scalability, allowing evolution of payloads and capabilities without fundamental platform redesign.
