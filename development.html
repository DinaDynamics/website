Platform Development Roadmap

Dina Dynamics is currently in active development of its autonomous hydrofoil platform.

Phase 1

Hydrodynamic Validation

Prototype testing of hydrofoil design, hull configuration, and power consumption characteristics. Focus on optimizing drag reduction and seakeeping performance.

Phase 2

Propulsion Integration

Development and integration of hybrid-electric propulsion system. Testing of diesel-electric and battery power modes under various operational conditions.

Phase 3

Autonomy Systems

Development of autonomous navigation stack, waypoint management, obstacle detection, and human operator interface. Integration of core autonomy algorithms.

Phase 4

Sensor Integration

Development of modular sensor payload systems. Testing of sensor data processing, real-time transmission to operators, and integration with autonomous functions.

Phase 5

Operational Validation

Field testing in realistic maritime environments. Demonstration of platform capability to defense and security organizations.

Development Focus Areas

Reliability & Durability

Extensive testing to ensure platform operates reliably in salt water, rough seas, and demanding maritime conditions. Redundancy in critical systems.

Human Operator Integration

Development of intuitive control interfaces and teleoperation systems. Ensuring operators can effectively monitor, direct, and intervene in platform operations.

Autonomous Capability

Building robust autonomous navigation, decision-making, and failsafe systems. Maintaining human oversight throughout all autonomous functions.
